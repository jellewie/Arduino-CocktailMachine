# Sponsorship of PCBWay
PCBWay kindly reached out to support our project after it caught their attention. They generously sponsored the production of three PCBs—Primary V2.2, Slot V2.2, and Dispenser V2.3—with one including full assembly. We covered the remaining costs for CNC parts, shipping, and taxes. We are (and never will) be paid to voice certain opinions, all options here are our own.

## Ordering Process
We were already planning to design these boards, and this process was not influenced by PCBWay. Although we might have gone with our usual supplier, we were excited to try out PCBWay. Their ordering system is somewhat more manual than what we're used to. For example, changing the silkscreen color requires navigating a less obvious setting, and other customizations need to be handled via customer service. Nonetheless, their support team was responsive and accommodating despite the time zone difference. Shipping was comparable to other manufacturers, with the boards securely packaged in bubble wrap inside a sturdy box. PCBWay also reached out via email to verify component selection and orientation, which we appreciated. Their attention to detail ensured everything was placed correctly.

## PCB Manufacturing
The parts came out great. The dimensions were spot on, the vias were tinted (looked almost plugged), and the silkscreen looked nice. The sizes of the letters were printed nicely and were readable. We opted for a black solder mask to give the boards a more professional appearance—PCBWay provided this at no extra charge, and the result looked great.

## PCB Assembly
As stated on their website, the assembly time takes a bit longer. We designed in EasyEDA and had some trouble converting the default BOM into the format that PCBWay requires. Once we understood the format and the stores PCBWay sources parts from, we had no further issues. The parts were placed accurately and soldered nicely. The assembled boards arrived with accurate component placement and clean soldering. Interestingly, even the SMD areas we had intended to solder ourselves were pre-soldered—but this was not an issue. 

## Extra Bonus: CNC Parts
We were pleased to learn that PCBWay also offers CNC machining services, which we used for custom stainless steel components. We specifically needed Stainless Steel 316 for its corrosion resistance and food safety compliance. While other suppliers either lacked the material or couldn’t meet our requirements, PCBWay handled the job without issue. The CNC parts matched our specifications exactly, including threaded sections as described in our drawings. Although CNC parts naturally have a longer turnaround time, PCBWay met our expectations—and succeeded where other providers could not.

## Conclusion
Overall, PCBWay's boards offer good value for the price and meet our project needs. We can recommend them.
